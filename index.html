How we work

Four stages. You know what is happening at each one, and nothing scales until it has earned it.

1

Audit

We look at the account, the tracking and the offer. If the maths cannot work at your margin, we say so before you spend anything.

2

Build

Account structure, conversion tracking, landing pages and the first round of creative. We fix measurement before we buy traffic.

3

Test

Small budgets across angles and audiences. Losers are cut on a fixed rule, not on a hunch about what should have worked.

4

Scale

Budget moves to what clears the target cost. We push until the cost per result rises, then hold and rebuild the test pipeline.

What you get judged on

These are the numbers in your weekly report. No impressions, no reach, no engagement rate.

Metric What it measures Why it matters
CPL Cost per lead that meets your qualification criteria, not every form fill. Tells you what a booked call actually costs you.
CPA Cost per completed sale, measured after refunds and cancellations. The only version of the number your accountant agrees with.
ROAS Revenue returned per unit of spend, on the channel and blended. Platform ROAS flatters itself. Blended does not.
MER Total revenue divided by total advertising spend across every channel. Shows whether growth in the account is real growth in the business.

We report against the target you set at the start. If a month misses it, the report says why and what changes next month.
